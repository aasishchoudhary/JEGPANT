# 100-mind research roster

## Status and interpretation

This is a **provisional engineering research roster**, not an objective ranking of IQ, genius, human worth, or scientific achievement. Real people and fictional characters cannot be measured on one common scale. Rank indicates the initial order in which JEGPANT will investigate candidate reasoning mechanisms—not a claim that rank 1 is universally more intelligent than rank 2.

- **[R]** real person or historical figure.
- **[F]** fictional character.
- **Mechanism** is the capability to investigate, not a personality to imitate.
- Every entry remains **proposed** until its attribution, evidence base, failure modes, and measurable implementation are reviewed.
- Patrick Jane is deliberately reserved for **#100**.

## How this list becomes engineering

Do not create 100 agents that role-play these names. Extract small, testable cognitive mechanisms and compose them into the system. For every mechanism, create a research card with:

- primary-source attribution and evidence class;
- operational definition and algorithmic representation;
- where the fictional portrayal diverges from evidence;
- prerequisites and operating conditions;
- failure modes, bias, and ethical limits;
- benchmark task, baseline, and pass criteria;
- implementation decision and measured results.

### Required challenge process

The roster itself must be challenged:
- Do not confuse fame, narrative intelligence, or omniscient storytelling with validated capability.
- Do not assume a person's historical reputation proves every attributed method.
- Do not infer that a character's success in a scripted story demonstrates real-world effectiveness.
- Do not copy criminal, coercive, exploitative, or unlawful conduct.
- Do not use body language or facial expression as a lie detector.
- Do not accept a capability until it survives independent tests and ablation.
